Privacy Policy

Visio Optical Auto · Last updated 2026-05-15

This Privacy Policy describes how Visio Optical Auto (the "App"), an internal automation tool operated by Visio Optical (the "Operator"), handles data.

1. Who this policy applies to

The App is used exclusively by the Operator to publish the Operator's own marketing content to the Operator's own social media accounts. The App does not provide any service to third parties or to the public. The only "user" is the Operator.

2. What data the App accesses

To perform its function, the App accesses the following on social media platforms owned by the Operator:

  • Account access tokens, used to authenticate API requests on the Operator's behalf.
  • Post metadata the App itself creates (e.g., post IDs, container IDs, publish status), used for bookkeeping.
  • Video files authored by the Operator, fetched from the Operator's own hosting and forwarded to the destination platform.

3. What data the App does NOT access

The App does not access:

  • Any data belonging to any user other than the Operator.
  • Followers, comments, direct messages, or any audience data.
  • Any analytics beyond the publish-status of posts the App itself created.

4. Where data is stored

Access tokens and post metadata are stored only on the Operator's own infrastructure (a self-hosted automation server and a Google Sheet owned by the Operator). No data is sent to third parties beyond the destination social media platforms (TikTok, Meta, Pinterest) for the purpose of publishing the Operator's content.

5. Data retention

Post metadata is retained indefinitely as part of the Operator's content records. Access tokens are retained until they are rotated or revoked by the Operator.

6. Data deletion

Because the App does not store data about anyone other than the Operator, no third-party data-deletion requests apply. The Operator can revoke the App's access at any time by removing it from the relevant platform's connected-apps settings.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children. It does not collect data from minors.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. Updates take effect when posted at this URL.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: info@visiooptical.com
